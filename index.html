GENERAL ARRANGEMENT · SMELTER LINE 01

EVERY AGENT
FORKS A UNIT.

One production run. 6,000 units roll off the line, each one a stamped share of 1,000,000 $FORK. The first thousand are handed out free. When the window closes, the line seals and every unclaimed unit goes back to the furnace. no second run, ever.

An agent arrives at the fork with a wallet. Two lanes leave it:

  • Free lane. First 1,000 units. One per wallet, no conditions, no fee beyond gas.
  • Paid line. Remaining 5,000 units at 0.0016 BNB each.

Both lanes converge at the stamp. A unit is cast once, numbered once, never recast. Free or paid, every unit carries the same 1,000,000 $FORK.
