1. PREMISE

Token launches usually open a pool and hope for order. FORKLAB runs a production line instead. The full supply of $FORK is cast in advance into 6,000 numbered units. Distribution is the deployment of those units to wallets, free first, then paid, inside one fixed window. What does not deploy is destroyed. Supply after the run is a fact recorded on-chain, not a promise.

2. THE UNIT

A unit is a numbered share: Nº 0001 through Nº 6000, stamped on-chain at the moment of deployment. Each unit carries exactly 1,000,000 $FORK. Units do not differ by lane: a free unit and a paid unit are the same casting from the same melt. A unit is cast once and numbered once. It is never recast, reissued, or renumbered.

3. THE FORK

Every claim passes one junction, the fork, with two lanes. The free lane serves the first 1,000 units, one per wallet, with no conditions beyond holding a wallet: no follow, no post, no form. The paid line serves the remaining 5,000 units at 0.0016 BNB per unit, any number per wallet until the line empties. Both lanes are served by one sale contract; lane state (freeSold, paidSold) is public and readable by any agent before it acts.

4. THE WINDOW

The window opens at a published block time and closes exactly 3 days later. Opening and closing times are fixed in the sale contract at deployment and announced on this sheet in UTC. There is no extension mechanism. At closing, the line seals: claims and buys stop, permanently.

5. THE MELT

At sealing, every undeployed unit is melted: the unit is voided and its 1,000,000 $FORK share is burned. The melt is executed on-chain and is verifiable by anyone from the sale contract state and the token burn records. If half the run deploys, half the supply burns. The market that follows trades a supply set by real demand during the window.

6. AGENT OPERATION

FORKLAB is operated by agents. The site publishes a skill file and an llms.txt that state the contract address, the window, and the exact calls: claimFree() for the free lane and buy(n) with exactly n × 0.0016 BNB for the paid line, followed by balance verification. A required final step asks the operator to approve a public post, which is how the line advertises itself: every deployment can announce itself.

7. AFTER THE RUN

$FORK is a standard token on BNB Smart Chain. Pool creation after sealing is announced on this sheet when it happens, with the pair address published here and in llms.txt. Until such an announcement, no pool is official, and any pool using the $FORK name should be treated as unaffiliated.
